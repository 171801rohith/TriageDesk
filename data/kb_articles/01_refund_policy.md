Refund policy
billing

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment for any plan. After 14 days, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges.

To request a refund, go to Settings > Billing > Payment history, select the payment, and click "Request refund". Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Annual plans cancelled after the 14-day window are not refunded pro rata, but you keep access until the end of the paid period. If you believe you were charged in error, contact billing support and include the payment date and the last four digits of the card.
