Resetting your password
account

If you forgot your password, open the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter the email address on your account and we will send a reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the address you signed up with. Company email filters sometimes block automated messages; ask your IT team to allow messages from our domain.

If you can no longer access the email address on your account, contact account support. For your security we will ask you to verify ownership before changing the email or password.
