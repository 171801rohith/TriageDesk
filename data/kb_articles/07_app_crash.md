Troubleshooting app crashes
technical

If the app crashes or closes on startup or during login, first make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. Many crashes are fixed in updates.

Next, restart your device and try again. If the app still crashes, clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > our app > Storage > Clear cache) or uninstall and reinstall the app (iOS). Your data is stored in your account, so reinstalling does not delete it.

If the crash continues, contact technical support with your device model, operating system version, app version (Settings > About), and the time the crash happened, so we can find the crash report. If the problem affects all users at once, check our status page for an ongoing outage.
