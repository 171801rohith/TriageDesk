Changing your account email
account

To change the email address on your account, go to Settings > Account > Email and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address; the change takes effect only after you click it. The link expires after 24 hours.

For security, we also send a notice to your old email address. If you did not request the change, use the link in that notice to cancel it and then reset your password.

Your login, receipts and notifications all move to the new address. If the new address is already used by another account, you need to delete or rename that account first.
