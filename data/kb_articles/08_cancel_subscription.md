Cancelling your subscription
billing

You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. After cancelling you keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again.

If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, you must cancel there instead, because those stores handle the billing. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Cancelling does not delete your data. Your account moves to the free plan, and you can resubscribe at any time to restore paid features. If you cancel within 14 days of your first payment you can also request a refund; see the refund policy.
