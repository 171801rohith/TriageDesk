Duplicate or double charges
billing

If you see two charges for the same subscription, the second one is usually a temporary authorization hold. Banks place a hold when a payment is retried, and the hold disappears on its own within 3 to 5 business days. It is not a completed payment.

If both charges are still on your statement after 5 business days, it is a real duplicate charge. Contact billing support with the dates and amounts of both charges and we will refund the duplicate in full, usually within 2 business days. You do not need to cancel your subscription.

To avoid duplicate charges, do not click "Pay" again if the payment page is slow; wait for the confirmation email instead.
