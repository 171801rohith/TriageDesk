Exporting your data
technical

You can download a copy of all your data at any time. Go to Settings > Privacy > Export data and click "Request export". We prepare a ZIP file containing your profile, projects and activity history in JSON and CSV formats.

Exports are usually ready within 1 hour, but large accounts can take up to 24 hours. We email you a download link when the file is ready. The link is valid for 7 days and requires you to be logged in.

Exporting your data does not delete or change anything in your account. If you want your data deleted, use Settings > Privacy > Delete account instead.
