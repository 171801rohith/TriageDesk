What to do if your package is lost
shipping

If your tracking page has not updated for 5 business days, or it shows "delivered" but you cannot find the package, first check with neighbours and your building's reception, and look for a delivery notice from the carrier.

If the package still cannot be found after 48 hours, contact shipping support with your order number. We will open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations take up to 5 business days.

If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. You do not need to pay for the replacement shipment.
