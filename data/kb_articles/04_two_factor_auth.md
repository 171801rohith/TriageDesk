Setting up two-factor authentication
account

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code to your login, so a stolen password alone cannot open your account. To turn it on, go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password.

When you enable 2FA we show ten backup codes. Store them somewhere safe; each code works once and lets you log in if you lose your phone.

If you lost your phone and your backup codes, contact account support. Recovery requires identity verification and can take up to 2 business days, because disabling 2FA without proof would defeat its purpose.
